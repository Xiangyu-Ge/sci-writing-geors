# 07 · 结论（Conclusion）

很多读者和审稿人会跳过正文直接看结论。它必须独立成立，又不能简单重复前文。

## 1. 形式

- 写成一段，100–200 词，比摘要更凝练
- 只有目标期刊惯用编号要点时，才改用编号（投稿前查 4 篇近作）

## 2. 内容

结论主要展现四件事：

1. 用了什么方法
2. 解决了什么问题
3. 主要发现
4. 对学科的贡献和意义

可按需补充应用前景和具体的下一步，但不写背景、不引文献、不引入新内容。

## 3. 结论、摘要、讨论的区别

| | 摘要 | 结论 | 讨论 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 角色 | 全文浓缩 | take-home 与贡献 | 解释与论证 |
| 背景 | 有 1 句 | 没有 | 按需 |
| 立场 | 中性陈述 | 价值声明 | 论证 |
| 不该有 | 引文献 | 重复讨论的细节论证 | 单纯重复结果 |

## 4. 示范（构造示例）

**定量反演**：
> *We developed a stacking ensemble that integrates Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 features to map topsoil salinity in an arid oasis, addressing the underestimation of high-salinity soils by single models. Validated against 120 independent field samples, the ensemble reduced RMSE by 18% relative to RF, and the resulting 10 m map showed that severe salinization was concentrated along low-lying oasis margins. Radar backscatter contributed most to the improvement at high salinity, where optical indices saturate. These results show that radar–optical fusion can extend salinity retrieval to heavily salinized soils, and the map provides a basis for targeting reclamation in the oasis.*

**时空变化**：
> *Using Theil–Sen trends and residual trend analysis of annual maximum NDVI for 2000–2022, we quantified vegetation change in an arid basin and separated the contributions of climate and human activities. Significant greening occurred across 63% of the basin, concentrated in the irrigated oasis, while browning was confined to the desert–oasis ecotone. Human activities accounted for 71% of the greening. This pixel-level attribution shows that irrigation expansion, rather than climate, has controlled recent vegetation change in the basin, which should be considered in water allocation between the oasis and the ecotone.*

## 5. 时态

| 内容 | 时态 |
|---|---|
| 本研究做了什么 | 过去简单 / 现在完成 |
| 具体结果 | 过去简单 |
| 结论性声明 | 现在简单 |
| 应用与展望 | 现在简单 + 情态动词 |

## 6. 收尾

最后一句要能被记住、被引用。三种收法：

- 重申核心贡献
- 落到应用或管理含义
- 呼应引言中的大背景

避免：*More research is needed.*；*Our study is limited and more work is needed.*；以 *In conclusion,* 开头（小标题已经说明了）。
