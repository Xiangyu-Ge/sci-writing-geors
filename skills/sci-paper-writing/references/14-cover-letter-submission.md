# 14 · Cover Letter 与投稿

Cover letter 写给主编，一页以内。它不重复摘要，只回答三件事：这篇讲什么、为什么适合本刊、有什么值得送审。主编通常先读标题、摘要和 cover letter，就决定送审还是拒稿。

## 1. 写作原则

- 主要发现直接、简洁地陈述，带核心数字；不写防御性的铺垫和解释
- 不用 novel、robust、comprehensive、unprecedented 这类评价词；贡献用事实说明
- 写清楚为什么投这本刊，并点名该刊读者为什么会关心
- 期刊名、主编称呼写对；群发痕迹（上一本刊的名字）会直接减分

## 2. 五要素

```
[1] 题目 + 稿件类型 + 期刊名
[2] 为什么适合本刊：主题落在 scope 内，该刊读者为何关心（可引该刊 1–2 篇相关论文）
[3] 做了什么、发现了什么：1–3 句，含核心数字
[4] 主要贡献：2–3 条，每条一个可验证的事实
[5] 标准声明 + 致谢：原创、未一稿多投、全体作者同意、无利益冲突、AI 使用声明（如适用）；推荐或回避审稿人（如期刊要求）
```

## 3. 示范（定量反演，投 Catena，构造示例）

> Dear Editor,
>
> We submit the manuscript entitled *"Mapping topsoil salinity by radar–optical fusion in an arid oasis, Northwest China"* for consideration as a research article in *CATENA*.
>
> Soil salinization is a major form of land degradation in arid regions, and mapping soil properties from multi-source remote sensing is within the scope of *CATENA*. The work builds on recent studies in the journal on salinity retrieval in drylands (refs).
>
> We integrated Sentinel-1 polarimetric features and Sentinel-2 spectral indices with 120 field-measured EC samples to map topsoil salinity across an oasis. The stacking ensemble achieved an R² of 0.81 (RMSE = 2.3 dS/m), 18% lower in RMSE than random forest, and reduced the underestimation of samples above 20 dS/m.
>
> The main contributions are:
> (1) radar features reduced the saturation of optical indices in heavily salinized soils;
> (2) a 10 m salinity map of the oasis, validated against independent samples;
> (3) a basis for targeting reclamation, as severe salinization covered 18% of the oasis.
>
> This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the submission and declare no competing interests. Thank you for considering our manuscript.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Corresponding author], on behalf of all authors

## 4. 高频句式

```
开头    We submit the manuscript entitled "..." for consideration as a research article in <Journal>.
scope   The study falls within the scope of <Journal>, which publishes work on...
        It builds on recent work published in <Journal> on... (refs)
发现    We found that... / The model achieved... / Human activities accounted for X% of...
声明    This manuscript is original and has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
        All authors have read and approved the manuscript.
        The authors declare no competing interests.
审稿人  We suggest the following potential reviewers: (name, affiliation, email, expertise)
收尾    Thank you for considering our manuscript.
```

## 5. 修回时的 cover letter

```
We thank the Editor and reviewers for their constructive comments. We have revised the manuscript accordingly; a point-by-point response is provided separately. The main changes are: (1)...; (2)...; (3).... All revisions are highlighted in the revised manuscript.
```

逐条回复审稿意见用 geo-reviewer-response skill。

## 6. 桌面拒稿的五道门与对策

稿件依次经过：自动技术检查 → 学术诚信筛查（查重、图像） → 编辑助理合规检查（字数、图表、声明） → 主编判断契合度与重要性 → 副编辑确认。顶刊的大部分稿件在送审前被拒，很多并非科研不行，而是触发了以下红线：

| 红线 | 对策 |
|---|---|
| 不符合期刊范围 | 读 aims & scope 和近 2–3 期；cover letter 里一句话点名契合；引该刊相关论文 |
| 新意不明 | 摘要、引言末尾、cover letter 三处显式写出 gap 与贡献 |
| 不符合作者须知 | 按 Guide for Authors 建提交清单逐条核对 |
| 语言与呈现弱 | 请团队外的同事读摘要和方法 |
| 方法含糊 | 写到同行能判断可复现性的程度 |
| 声明缺失 | 利益冲突、数据可得性、作者贡献、AI 使用声明都写，哪怕是"无" |

遥感期刊的初筛问题见 `00-journal-targeting.md` §2。

## 7. 投稿材料清单

手稿（含图表）、cover letter、Highlights（Elsevier 刊 3–5 条，每条 ≤ 85 字符）、Graphical Abstract、基金信息、数据可得性声明、利益冲突声明、作者贡献（CRediT）、推荐/回避审稿人、AI 使用声明（如使用了 AI 辅助）。

**投稿前 24 小时终检**：再读一遍 Guide for Authors；确认全体合著者批准终版；以时间很少的编辑的视角重读标题和摘要前两句；确认 cover letter 中的期刊名正确；确认参考文献没有被撤稿的；确认没有同时投他刊。

## 8. 自查清单

- [ ] 一页以内，期刊名和称呼正确
- [ ] 一句话说清为什么适合本刊
- [ ] 核心发现直接陈述，带数字，没有防御性铺垫
- [ ] 贡献 2–3 条，每条是可验证的事实，没有评价形容词
- [ ] 声明齐全（含 AI 使用声明，如适用）
- [ ] 没有照抄摘要
