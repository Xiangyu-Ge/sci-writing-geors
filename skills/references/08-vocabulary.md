# 08 · 高频词汇与句式总库

本文件是 SCI 写作的"工具箱"——当用户卡在"这里怎么说"时,**直接来这里查**。所有词汇均从 2500+ 篇 STEMM 顶刊已发表论文中提取,确认是当前编辑/读者接受的"标准用法"。

## 使用方法

- 按**写作功能**(我现在想表达什么)查,不要按字母表查
- 选词时**注意词性**(动词 vs 名词 vs 形容词 vs 副词)
- 同一概念在全文中**保持一致**,不要"换近义词"
- 看不准时,把候选词扔进 Google Scholar 用引号搜,看真实使用频率和语境

## 10. 因果关系动词(确定性梯度)

按"因果强度"从强到弱排列:

```
强(直接因果):
  cause, produce, be due to, result in, result from, give rise to

中(部分原因):
  contribute to, be a factor in, play a role in

弱(过程性,间接):
  lead to, drive, govern, accompany

中性(连接但不指明因果):
  be associated with, be connected to, be related to, be linked to,
  be ascribed to, be attributed to
```

### 表语 / 介词搭配的隐含信息

- *X is **a** cause of Y* → 还有其他原因
- *X is **the** cause of Y* → X 是唯一原因
- *X **results from** Y* → X 是结果,Y 是原因
- *X **results in** Y* → Y 是结果,X 是原因

## 11. 推测 / 含义动词(确定性梯度)

```
最强 ──────────────────────────────────────── 最弱
prove / establish / demonstrate

```

### 配合模态动词

```
强:      will, must, has to, is sure to, certainly does
中强:     should, ought to, would, is likely to
中:       can, may
中弱:     could, might
弱:       may possibly, might conceivably
推测:     could potentially, may speculatively
```

### 完整推测语模板

```
[Hedge] [verb] [hedge] that [claim].

例:
Our results suggest that X may be related to Y.
It appears therefore that X probably affects Y.
The evidence points to the possibility that X may contribute to Y.
These findings indicate that X is likely associated with Y.
```

## 12. 问题 / 局限 / 缓和语

### 12.1 承认局限的开头

```
A potential limitation of this study is that...
We acknowledge that...
It should be noted that...
A caveat to this interpretation is that...
We note, however, that...
Although our results... they should be interpreted with caution because...
This finding requires confirmation in larger / independent / future studies.
```

### 12.2 缓和(hedging)语

```
状语:
seemingly, apparently, presumably, ostensibly,
probably, possibly, likely, in some / most cases,
to some extent, to a (large / small) degree

动词短语:
appear to, seem to, tend to, be likely to,
be expected to, be thought to, be reported to

形容词:
likely, possible, potential, plausible, probable, suggestive

副词:
arguably, perhaps, conceivably
```

### 12.3 用 hedging 控制语气

| 不缓和(过强) | 加 hedging(刚好) |
|---|---|
| *X causes Y.* | *X **may** cause Y.* |
| *Our method is the best.* | *Our method **appears to** outperform existing approaches in this benchmark.* |
| *This proves that...* | *This **provides strong evidence** that...* |

## 13. 连接词与信号词

### 13.1 因 / 果 / 对比 / 出乎意料 / 增添 / 转换

| 功能 | 词 / 短语 | 注意 |
|---|---|---|
| **因** | due to (the fact that), on account of, in view of (the fact that), as, because, since, owing to | `as` 也表"当...时";`since` 也表"自从";有歧义时换词 |
| **果** | therefore, consequently, hence, thus, as a result, accordingly, so | `therefore` 后必须是上句**直接**结果 |
| **对比 / 差异** | however, whereas, while, by contrast, in contrast, on the other hand, but | `while` 也表"同时";`conversely / on the contrary` 表"相反"(更强,只有对立时才用) |
| **出乎意料** | although, even though, though (从句); despite, in spite of, regardless of, notwithstanding (短语); nevertheless, nonetheless, yet, even so, however (独立句) | `however / but` 也表对比,语气更轻 |
| **增添** | in addition, moreover, furthermore, also, secondly, what is more, besides | `besides` 语气更强,劝说语境用 |
| **转换话题** | with regard to, with respect to, regarding, turning now to, as for, as to | — |
| **举例** | for example, for instance, e.g., specifically, in particular, namely, that is, i.e. | `e.g.` vs `i.e.`:前者举例,后者改述 |
| **总结** | in summary, in short, in brief, to summarise, taken together, collectively, overall, on balance | — |
| **重述** | in other words, that is to say, namely, i.e. | — |
| **强调** | indeed, in fact, importantly, notably, crucially, of note | 慎用 `clearly / obviously` — 暗示读者不聪明 |

### 13.2 句首 / 句中 / 句尾的位置

很多 connector 既可放句首也可放句中。位置改变重心:

- *Unfortunately, in such cases, the patient may require a higher dose...* (强调"很遗憾")
- *In such cases, the patient may require a higher dose, unfortunately.* (弱化,读完才知道)

**通常长句把 connector 放在句首**,短句两者都可。

### 13.3 慎用规则

- **不要每句都用 connector**。文章会变得机械化、急促。
- **替代方案**:重复关键词 / 用代词 + 名词("This method, These findings")
- **首句不要用 *Moreover / Furthermore*** —— 因为还没有"前一句"

## 14. 频率与数量限定词

### 14.1 频率(出现的频率)

| 强 → 弱 | 词 |
|---|---|
| 100% | *always, invariably, without exception* |
| ~90% | *usually, typically, normally, generally, predominantly* |
| ~70% | *often, frequently, commonly* |
| ~50% | *sometimes, occasionally, periodically* |
| ~30% | *seldom, rarely, infrequently* |
| 0% | *never, in no case* |

### 14.2 数量 / 程度

| 强 → 弱 | 词 |
|---|---|
| 全部 | *all, every, in all cases, universally* |
| 大多数 | *most, the majority of, in most cases, predominantly* |
| 许多 | *many, numerous, a large number of* |
| 一些 | *some, several, a number of, in some cases* |
| 少数 | *a few, a small number of, only a handful* |
| 几乎没有 | *almost no, hardly any, very few* |
| 没有 | *none, no, in no case* |

### 14.3 用频率词软化绝对句

❌ *X causes Y.* (太绝对)
✅ *X **usually / often / typically** causes Y.* (合理软化)

❌ *Our method outperforms all existing methods.* (审稿人会逼你证明所有方法都试过)
✅ *Our method **outperforms most existing methods** evaluated in this study.* (限定范围,可证)

## 最后一条建议

**Hilary Glasman-Deal 的原话:"The thesaurus is not your friend, and it is the global writer and reader's enemy. No two words have exactly the same meaning in every context, so there is no such thing as a perfect synonym."**

中文用户特别容易掉进"换词"的陷阱(怕重复显得 vocabulary 不丰富)。**在 SCI 写作里,重复用同一个关键词是好事**——它让概念稳定,让读者轻松追踪。

# ★ 地理 / 遥感领域专属词库(在通用词库之上叠加)

> 以上为通用学术词库,普适;以下为地理/遥感(尤其定量反演与时空变化)的专属表达。卡在"领域里怎么说"时来这里查。

## 15. 研究区描述(Study Area)
```
位置: is located in / lies in / is situated in; covers ~X km²; extends from X°N to Y°N
气候: arid / semi-arid / temperate continental / monsoon; mean annual temperature; mean annual precipitation; potential evaporation far exceeds precipitation
地形: flat / undulating / mountainous; elevation ranges from X to Y m a.s.l.; the terrain
水文: is drained by the X River; fed by snowmelt; shallow groundwater table (< X m); irrigation return flow
土壤: dominant soil types are (Solonchaks / Aridisols); according to WRB / USDA / Chinese Soil Taxonomy
植被: dominated by (halophytes / desert shrubs / grassland); sparse vegetation; salt-tolerant species
收敛句: is highly prone to (salinization / degradation); a representative / typical / ideal site for studying...
```

## 16. 遥感数据与传感器(Data)
```
卫星/传感器: Sentinel-1 (C-band SAR), Sentinel-2 (MSI), Landsat-5/7/8/9 (TM/ETM+/OLI), MODIS, GF-1/2/5, ASTER, hyperspectral (PRISMA, EnMAP)
产品级别: Level-1C top-of-atmosphere, Level-2A surface reflectance, GRD, SLC
参数: spatial resolution (10 m / 30 m / 250 m); spectral resolution; spectral bands / band range; revisit / temporal resolution; swath
预处理: atmospheric correction, geometric / orthorectification, radiometric calibration, cloud / shadow masking, speckle filtering (SAR), topographic correction, compositing, gap-filling
平台/来源: Google Earth Engine (GEE), Copernicus Open Access Hub, USGS Earth Explorer, NASA Earthdata
辅助数据: digital elevation model (DEM; SRTM / ASTER GDEM); land-use/land-cover (LULC); meteorological / ERA5 reanalysis; soil database
实测: in-situ / field-measured; topsoil (0–20 cm); sampling sites; stratified random / grid sampling; electrical conductivity (EC); portable EC meter; ASD spectroradiometer; oven-dried
```

## 17. 指数 / 变量 / 特征(常用名词,保持术语一致)
```
光谱指数: NDVI, EVI, SAVI, MSAVI, NDWI, NDSI, salinity index (SI), brightness index
雷达特征: backscatter coefficient (σ0), VV / VH polarization, polarimetric features, coherence
地形协变量: elevation, slope, aspect, topographic wetness index (TWI)
纹理: GLCM texture (contrast, entropy, homogeneity)
```

## 18. 定量反演:建模与精度验证语言
```
建模: train / calibrate / fit / develop a model; predictors / covariates / response variable; pixel-by-pixel / wall-to-wall prediction
变量筛选: variable importance, recursive feature elimination (RFE), Boruta, Pearson correlation, VIF, collinearity
算法: partial least squares regression (PLSR), random forest (RF), gradient boosting (XGBoost), support vector regression (SVR), Cubist, convolutional neural network (CNN)
验证方式: k-fold / 10-fold / leave-one-out cross-validation; independent validation set; training/validation split (70/30)
精度指标: coefficient of determination (R²), root-mean-square error (RMSE), mean absolute error (MAE), bias, ratio of performance to deviation (RPD), Lin's concordance correlation
报告句: achieved an R² of X and an RMSE of Y; outperformed the baseline; predicted values closely followed the 1:1 line; slight over-/under-estimation at (high/low) values
不确定性: prediction interval, bootstrap, uncertainty was quantified by...
```

## 19. 时空变化:趋势 / 变化检测 / 归因语言
```
长时序: time series, annual maximum/mean composite, max-NDVI compositing, harmonic fitting, anomaly
趋势: linear trend / slope, Theil–Sen (Sen's) median slope, Mann–Kendall (MK) test, trend significance (p < 0.05), increasing/decreasing trend, greening/browning
变化检测: change detection, image differencing, post-classification comparison, change vector analysis (CVA), LandTrendr, BFAST, breakpoint / change point
分级/统计: significantly increased/decreased, no significant change; accounted for X% of the area; expanded / shrank by X%; rate of change per year
驱动归因: driving factors / drivers, attribution, residual trend analysis, partial correlation, multiple regression, geographical detector (geodetector), relative contribution of climate vs human activities (X% and Y%)
表述: was associated with / can be attributed to / was largely driven by / may be partly explained by (见 09 确定性连续谱)
```

## 20. 地理/遥感"happy words"与价值词
```
multi-source / multi-sensor / multi-temporal     high-resolution / fine-resolution / 10 m
radar–optical fusion                             wall-to-wall / spatially explicit
first long-term / first regional / first wall-to-wall   transferable / generalizable
operational / scalable / cost-effective          robust / reliable / accurate / quantitative
```

## 21. 单位与写法(地理/遥感约定)
```
温度 °C / K;距离 m, km;面积 ha, km²;海拔 m a.s.l.
盐分 EC dS/m (或 mS/cm);SOC g/kg 或 %;土壤水分 m³/m³ 或 vol.%
分辨率 10 m / 30 m / 250 m;时段写 2000–2022(en dash,不是 hyphen)
R² 报两位小数;p 值 p < 0.05;斜率给单位(slope = 0.003 yr⁻¹)
首次出现全称+缩写;全文术语一致(NDVI 不要又写成 the vegetation index)
```
