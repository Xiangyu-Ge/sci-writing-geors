# 11 · 投稿信(Cover Letter)写作详解 · 地理/遥感版

Cover letter 是写给**主编(Editor-in-Chief / Handling Editor)**的一封短信,随稿件一起提交。它不重复摘要,而是回答主编最关心的三件事:**这篇讲什么、为什么适合本刊、有什么亮点值得送审**。一页以内,礼貌、自信、具体。

## 1. 🍳 配方:5 要素(本技能默认结构)

```
[1] 题目 + 稿件类型:写明文章标题、投稿的期刊、稿件类别(research article / letter)
[2] 是否适合本刊:一句话说明研究主题为什么落在该刊范围(scope)内、为什么该刊读者会关心
[3] 做了什么:1-3 句概括研究对象、数据/方法、核心发现(贡献=磁极)
[4] 亮点:2-4 条,讲清楚新意(首次?多源?长时序?高精度?可操作?)
[5] 致谢 + 标准声明:感谢主编考虑;声明原创、未一稿多投、所有作者同意投稿;(可选)推荐/回避审稿人
```

## 2. 完整 Cover Letter 示范(构造示例,定量反演)

> Dear Editor,
>
> **[1 题目+类型]** We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled *"Mapping topsoil salinity by radar–optical fusion in an arid oasis, Northwest China"* for consideration as a **research article** in *CATENA*.
>
> **[2 是否适合]** Soil salinization is one of the most widespread forms of land degradation in arid regions, and the operational mapping of soil properties from multi-source remote sensing falls squarely within the scope of *CATENA*. We believe this work will be of interest to the journal's readership in soil science and land degradation.
>
> **[3 做了什么]** In this study, we developed a multi-source retrieval framework that integrates Sentinel-1 polarimetric features and Sentinel-2 spectral indices with field-measured electrical conductivity to map topsoil salinity across an arid oasis. The model achieved an R² of 0.81 (RMSE = 2.3 dS/m), substantially outperforming optical-only approaches.
>
> **[4 亮点]** The main contributions of this work are:
> - We demonstrate that **radar–optical fusion** overcomes the vegetation limitation of optical salinity retrieval.
> - We produce the **first wall-to-wall, 10 m salinity map** of the basin.
> - The framework provides an **operational basis** for regional salinization monitoring and precision reclamation.
>
> **[5 声明+致谢]** This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have read and approved the submission and declare no competing interests. We thank you for your time and consideration, and look forward to your response.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Corresponding author name], on behalf of all authors
> [Affiliation, email]

## 3. 时空变化版的措辞差异([3][4] 替换)
> **[3 做了什么]** We quantified per-pixel vegetation dynamics across the basin from 2000 to 2022 and attributed the trends to climatic and anthropogenic drivers. Significant greening occurred across 63% of the basin, of which 71% was attributable to human activities.
> **[4 亮点]**
> - First **pixel-level, long-term (2000–2022)** attribution of vegetation change in this arid basin.
> - We disentangle the **relative contributions of climate and human activities** using residual trend analysis.
> - Findings provide **direct implications for water and land management** in arid oases.

## 4. 高频句式库
```
开头/题目
We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "..." for consideration as a research article in <Journal>.
Please find enclosed our manuscript "...", which we wish to submit to <Journal>.

是否适合本刊(scope)
This study falls within the scope of <Journal>, which publishes work on...
We believe this work will be of interest to the journal's readership in...
Given the journal's focus on (soil science / remote sensing / land degradation), we consider it a suitable outlet.

做了什么(贡献)
In this study, we developed/quantified/mapped...
Our main finding is that...; the model achieved...; we attributed X% to...

亮点(highlights)
The main contributions of this work are: ... / This manuscript is novel in that...
To our knowledge, this is the first... / We provide the first wall-to-wall / long-term...

标准声明
This manuscript is original and has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
All authors have read and approved the manuscript and agree to its submission.
The authors declare no conflict of interest.
(可选)We have no objection to the data being made available...

审稿人推荐/回避(若期刊要求)
We suggest the following potential reviewers: ... (name, affiliation, email, expertise)
We respectfully request that the following individuals not review this manuscript, owing to potential conflicts of interest: ...

致谢/收尾
We thank you for considering our manuscript and look forward to your response.
Sincerely, / Yours faithfully,
```

## 5. 修回时的 Cover Letter(revision,简版)
返修(minor/major revision)再投时,cover letter 要简短说明:
```
We thank the Editor and reviewers for their constructive comments.
We have carefully revised the manuscript accordingly; a point-by-point response is provided separately.
The main changes include: (1)...; (2)...; (3)...
All revisions are highlighted in the revised manuscript.
```
> 逐条回应放在单独的 "Response to Reviewers" 文件里,cover letter 只做总览(详见 `10-checklist.md` M 节)。

## 6. 实操写作清单
- [ ] **一页以内**,称呼正确(Dear Editor / Dear Dr. X)
- [ ] 题目、期刊名、稿件类型写对
- [ ] 一句话说清**为什么适合本刊(scope)**
- [ ] 贡献(磁极)用 1-3 句讲清,核心数字在(反演:R²/RMSE;变化:趋势/归因比例)
- [ ] 亮点 2-4 条,每条一个真实卖点(首次/多源/长时序/高精度/可操作)
- [ ] **标准声明齐全**:原创、未一稿多投、所有作者同意、无利益冲突
- [ ] (若期刊要求)推荐/回避审稿人信息完整
- [ ] 致谢 + 落款(通讯作者 + 单位 + 邮箱)
- [ ] 语气**自信但不浮夸**,无 *preliminary / some considerations* 这类弱词

## 7. 常见错误
- **照抄摘要**:cover letter 不是摘要复制版;它是写给主编的"为什么值得送审"。
- **没说 scope**:不解释为什么投这本刊 → 主编第一反应是 desk reject。
- **亮点太多太弱**:列 8 条流水账 → 只留 2-4 条最强的。
- **过度自夸**:堆 *novel, robust, comprehensive, unique, unprecedented* → 挑准的,用数字撑。
- **漏声明**:忘了"未一稿多投/所有作者同意" → 部分期刊视为不合规。
- **称呼/期刊名出错**:把 *CATENA* 写成别的刊 → 显得在群发,直接减分。

## 8. 🍳 配方速用版 vs 🔬 反向工程版
- **🍳 配方**:5 要素直接套(§1-3),够用于绝大多数投稿。
- **🔬 反向工程**:目标期刊官网 Guide for Authors 常规定 cover letter 必含项(如 data availability、suggested reviewers、novelty statement)。投前读一遍,按该刊要求增删。
