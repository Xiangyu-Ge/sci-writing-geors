# 05 · 结论(Conclusion)写作详解 · 地理/遥感版

结论是**全文最被读的几个部分之一**——很多读者(和审稿人)会跳过正文直接看结论。它必须**独立成立**(读完就知道 take-home),又不能简单重复前文。

## 1. 位置与字数
四种安排:独立 Conclusion 段;嵌入 Discussion 末尾;Results and Discussion / Conclusion;Results / Conclusion。
**字数**:通常 **100-200 词**,1-2 个短段落,偶尔用编号要点列"主要发现"。让用户先数目标期刊(Catena/Geoderma 偏好编号要点;RSE 偏简短段落)。

## 2. 结论的 11 要素菜单(按需选取)
```
1·论文内容    2·成就/贡献    3·背景    4·gap/目标    5·方法
6·关键结果+评价  7·结果含义  8·局限  9·应用  10·如何推进知识  11·未来方向
```
**地理/遥感最常见组合**:2 + 6 + 7 + 9 + 11(成就 + 关键结果 + 含义 + 应用 + 未来)。

## 3. 结论 ≠ 摘要 ≠ 讨论收尾
| 元素 | 摘要 | 结论 | 讨论 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 角色 | 全文浓缩 | take-home + 影响 | 解读+论证 |
| 长度 | 150-250 词 | 100-200 词 | 全文 25-35% |
| 立场 | 中性陈述 | 价值声明+推荐 | 论证 |
| 时态 | 现在简单+过去简单 | 现在简单/现在完成为主 | 多种混合 |
| 不该有 | 强烈推荐 | 重复讨论的细节论证 | 单纯重复结果 |

## 4. 时态
| 描述对象 | 时态 |
|---|---|
| 论文做了什么 | 现在简单/现在完成(*We present... / We have developed...*) |
| 主要结果(声明) | 现在简单(*Human activities are the dominant driver.*) |
| 具体结果(本研究) | 过去简单(*The model achieved an R² of 0.81.*) |
| 应用/含义 | 现在简单+模态(*This could support... / has implications for...*) |
| 未来工作 | should / will / could |

## 5. 结构模板
**A·浓缩型(≤100 词)**:[1] 本文做了 X(happy words) [2] 关键结果/贡献 [3] 含义/应用/未来
**B·展开型(150-200 词)**:[1] 重申本文(呼应引言) [2-3] 关键结果+评价+与知识的关系 [4-5] 含义/应用 [末] 未来工作/强力收尾

## 6. 开头七式
```
1. We have developed / presented / mapped / quantified...
2. In this study, we...
3. This study presents / reports / develops...
4. The aim of this study was to... We have shown that...
5. To our knowledge, this is the first...
6. In summary, ...
7. Taken together, our results show that...
```

## 7. 双分支示范

### 【定量反演】结论(构造示例,~120 词)
> **[本文做了什么+happy words]** *In this study, we developed a **robust multi-source framework** that integrates Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data for retrieving topsoil salinity in an arid oasis.*
> **[关键结果]** *The model achieved an R² of 0.81 and an RMSE of 2.3 dS/m, **outperforming** optical-only approaches, with radar backscatter contributing substantially to the improvement.*
> **[贡献+制图]** *We produced the **first wall-to-wall, 10 m salinity map** of the basin, revealing severe salinization along the low-lying oasis margins.*
> **[应用+未来]** *The approach **provides an operational basis** for regional salinization monitoring and precision reclamation. **Future work will** test its transferability to other arid basins and incorporate hyperspectral data to reduce saturation at high salinity.*

### 【时空变化】结论(构造示例,~120 词)
> **[本文做了什么]** *We quantified per-pixel vegetation dynamics across the basin from 2000 to 2022 and attributed the trends to climatic and anthropogenic drivers.*
> **[关键结果+评价]** *Significant greening occurred across **63% of the basin**, concentrated within the irrigated oasis, whereas browning was confined to the desert–oasis ecotone.*
> **[归因/认知贡献]** *Residual trend analysis attributed **71% of the greening to human activities** and 29% to climate, **indicating that** irrigation expansion, rather than climate change, dominated the trend.*
> **[含义+未来]** *These findings **have direct implications for** sustainable water and land management in arid oases. **Future studies should** examine whether this human-driven greening is sustainable under intensifying drought.*

## 8. 收尾句——一锤定音(三路线)
- **A 展望+影响**:*We anticipate that this framework will support operational salinization monitoring across global drylands.*
- **B 重申核心贡献**:*In summary, this work provides the first long-term, pixel-level attribution of vegetation change in the basin.*
- **C 呼应大背景**:*Given the accelerating salinization of irrigated drylands worldwide, the approach offers a scalable path toward early monitoring.*
> **避免**空洞收尾 *"More research is needed."*

## 9. 实操写作清单
- [ ] 字数符合目标期刊(数过 3-5 篇)?编号要点 or 段落,与期刊一致?
- [ ] **没有重复**讨论的细节论证?
- [ ] take-home 在**前两句**出现?
- [ ] 反演:报了核心精度 + 制图贡献?变化:报了趋势 + 归因结论?
- [ ] 用了 happy words(节制)?
- [ ] 没引入新内容?
- [ ] 有明确"未来方向"或"应用前景"(且区分 should vs will)?
- [ ] 最后一句够有力?
- [ ] 独立成立(剥离正文还看得懂)?

## 10. 常见错误
- **把摘要复制到结论**:摘要含背景,结论不该含;读者一眼识破。
- **重复讨论的具体论证**:结论里浓缩成一句(*Our findings extend...*)。
- **过度谦虚**:❌ *Our study is limited and more work is needed.* → ✅ *Despite its limitations, this study provides the first...*
- **"In conclusion," 开头**:太套路,小标题已说明是结论。
- **画饼**:❌ *In the future, we will revolutionize salinity monitoring.* → ✅ 具体可行的下一步。
- **没让人记住的那句**:至少有一句能被引用、能被记住(*Human activities, not climate, drove the recent greening of this arid oasis.*)。

## 11. 🍳 配方速用版 vs 🔬 反向工程版
- **🍳 配方**:用组合 2+6+7+9+11,按反演/变化分支套 §7 示范。够投 Catena/Geoderma/JAG/IJRS。
- **🔬 反向工程**:冲 RSE/ISPRS J 时,数 4 篇近作:结论是段落还是编号?多少词?有没有应用/政策含义句?照它校准。
